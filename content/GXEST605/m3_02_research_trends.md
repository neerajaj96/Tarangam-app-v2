# Research: Market, Consumer & Tech Trends

**Evidence-fed imagination — desk/field research, trend radar, and cross-industry theft done right.**

<a id="the-intuition"></a>
## 1. The Intuition

::: callout-intuition Core Mental Model: Detective's Corkboard
**Market research** (sizes/growth/segments/competitors — TAM-SAM-SOM honesty!). **Consumer research** (habits/wallets/jobs-to-be-done — purchase logic, not demographics alone!). **Tech trends** (radar: adopt/trial/assess/hold rings! — emerging-tech maturity vs hype!). **Cross-industry inspiration** (hospital triage → canteen queues! airlines → hospital boarding! — Mechanism-transplant, not metaphor-magic!). Corkboard pins evidence *behind* every spark (research-fed, not research-theatre!).
:::

<a id="the-math"></a>
## 2. Theoretical Framework & Formalism

### 2.1 Research menu + trend radar + transplant discipline

* Market (secondary reports + sizing math + competitor teardowns!). Consumer (surveys + diaries + jobs-theory interviews!). Tech (radar rings + TRL cross-check M1.3! + integration readiness!).
* Cross-industry (mechanism extraction: *what* transfers + *boundary conditions*! — triage≠transplant blindly!).
* Leveraging rule (evidence-per-idea: sparks cite pins!).

::: callout-formula KTU Formula Vault: Research
Size (TAM-SAM-SOM!) · habits (jobs!) · radar (rings!) · transplants (mechanisms+boundaries!).
:::

::: callout-pitfall Cargo-Cult Transplants (Airlines Painted on Hospitals!)
Surface copying without mechanism/boundary analysis (boarding-groups need *called* cohorts — hospital lacks PA discipline!) — transplant audits (mechanism + preconditions + adaptation plan!) precede imitation.
:::

<a id="worked-example"></a>
## 3. Worked Example / Step-by-Step Scenario

::: step [Step 1: Setup] Formulating the Problem
"QR-token canteen idea (M3.1 winner!): (a) Market sizing sketch? (b) Consumer probe trio? (c) One cross-industry transplant with boundary audit? (d) Trend radar placement (QR payments!)?"
:::

::: step [Step 2: Execution] Evidence Behind the Spark
1. (a) TAM (all campus eaters × transaction!) → SAM (queue-plagued counters!) → SOM (pilot canteen term-1!) — honesty shrinking, numbers attached.
2. (b) Phone-comfort? UPI-trust? Peak-flexibility? (adoption blockers probed, not assumed!).
3. (c) Airport boarding groups → counter time-slots: mechanism (called cohorts smooth flow!) + boundary (PA system + captive audience *absent* in canteens — token-display adaptation planned!).
4. (d) QR/UPI: adopt ring (mature rails!) — trend tailwind cited, not chased.
:::

::: step [Step 3: Conclusion] Final Result
Sized market, probed consumers, audited transplant, ringed trend. Evidence-per-spark (pins behind ideas!) is the research-fed standard.
:::

## 4. Active Recall Quizzes

::: quiz Q1: Foundational Concept
TAM-SAM-SOM shrinking discipline prevents:
(A) Small thinking (ambition intact — staged!).
(*B) Fantasy scaling (TAM-quoted-as-target! — SOM honesty (reachable beachhead!) stages growth believably; investor-grade arithmetic!)
(C) Market research altogether
(D) Niche domination (beachheads *enable* it!).
::: explanation
Funnel honesty (universe→reachable→winnable!) stages claims (beachhead first!). TAM-as-target is the pitch fallacy — SOM-grounded plans survive diligence.
:::

::: quiz Q2: Foundational Concept
Jobs-to-be-done beats demographics for consumer research since:
(A) Demographics illegal now
(*B) Hiring-logic (progress sought in circumstances!) predicts purchase across ages/incomes (same job, diverse buyers!) — causality over correlation (circumstance-driven design!).
(C) Surveys dislike ages
(D) Jobs are trendier wording
::: explanation
Circumstance-causality (struggling moments hire products!) segments by *job* (commuter-calm-seekers cross ages!). Job-story format (when-I-want-help-me-so-I-can!) focuses design on progress, not profiles.
:::

::: quiz Q3: Foundational Concept
Tech-radar rings (adopt/trial/assess/hold) govern:
(A) Procurement brands only
(*B) Maturity-matched commitment (adopt: proven rails!; trial: bounded pilots!; assess: lab spikes!; hold: deliberate pause with revisit dates!) — ring-appropriate bets (no production bets on assess-ring tech!).
(C) Hiring stacks
(D) Conference agendas
::: explanation
Commitment calibration (bet size ∝ maturity!) — ring discipline prevents hype-driven production gambles *and* laggard paralysis. Dated revisits (hold≠forever!) keep radar alive.
:::
