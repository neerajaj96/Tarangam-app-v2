## 4. Active Recall Quizzes

::: quiz Q1: Mixed Drill
Six hats on a *solo* founder (no team). Adaptation?
(A) Skip hats (needs groups!)
(*B) Timed solo rounds (hat-timer per mode — forced perspective rotation solo!; journal per hat for audit!) — discipline replaces group dynamics (structure over headcount!)
(C) Only green hat (favourite bias!)
(D) Hats need $6$ people (myth!)
::: explanation
Solo-hatting protocol (timed forced-modes + written traces!) preserves parallel-thinking benefits minus group friction. Structure-scales-down (rituals > headcounts!) is the facilitation insight.
:::

::: quiz Q2: Mixed Drill
SWOT vs decision matrix placement (both analytical!): when which?
(A) Interchangeable always
(*B) SWOT maps terrain (internal/external audit *before* options firm!); matrix scores *committed* finalists (options ripe!) — sequence: SWOT early (landscape!), matrix late (verdict!) — stage-matched tools
(C) Matrix first always
(D) SWOT replaces research
::: explanation
Audit-then-verdict sequencing (terrain before scoring!) — premature matrices score fog (criteria float without landscape!). Stage-matched tooling (map-then-measure!) orders analysis.
:::

::: quiz Q3: Mixed Drill
Feasibility says market-PASS but ops-KILL (no maintainer exists). Verdict?
(A) Proceed (market rules!)
(*B) Kill-or-redesign-for-ops (unmaintained products die on schedule — ops veto stands unless maintenance invented: community contracts! service model pivot!) — veto honoured or design-around proven
(C) Downplay ops (launch will fix!)
(D) Average the lenses (compensatory fallacy on red lines!)
::: explanation
Veto-honouring (red is red!) with design-around optionality (invent maintenance, then re-verdict!). Averaging-vetoes fallacy named and refused — governance spine intact.
:::
