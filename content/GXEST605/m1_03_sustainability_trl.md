# Sustainability & Technology Readiness Levels

**Triple-bottom-line design plus the 1–9 honesty scale for "how ready is this really?"**

<a id="the-intuition"></a>
## 1. The Intuition

::: callout-intuition Core Mental Model: Grandchildren's Audit + Honesty Thermometer
**Sustainability** designs for three bottom lines (planet: materials/energy/end-of-life!; people: equity/safety/livelihoods!; profit: viable without subsidies-forever!) — grandchildren audit today's choices (e-waste, carbon, extraction!). **TRL** thermometers tech maturity ($1$ principles observed → $4$ lab validation → $6$ relevant demo → $9$ proven operations!) — funding/design reviews calibrate by level (lab-curiosity vs field-ready distinguished! valley-of-death between $4$–$7$ named!).
:::

---

<a id="the-math"></a>
## 2. Theoretical Framework & Formalism

### 2.1 Triple lenses + TRL rungs + lifecycle placement

* Environmental (footprint, circularity: reduce/reuse/recycle/refuse hierarchies!), economic (total-cost incl. externalities!), social (equity, livelihoods, cultural fit!).
* TRL $1$–$3$ (research!), $4$–$6$ (development/demo!), $7$–$9$ (deploy/prove!) — evidence per rung (papers→prototypes→pilots→operations!).
* Placement: TRL gates funding/design effort (low-TRL = research money, high-TRL = scale money — mismatch wastes both!).

::: callout-formula KTU Formula Vault: Sustain + Ready
Planet/people/**profit triple** · TRL **$1$-principles → $9$-proven** · money **matches rung**.
:::

::: callout-pitfall Greenwashing by Vagueness ("Eco-Friendly!" Stickers!)
Unmeasured claims (no lifecycle numbers!) are marketing (Scope-$1$/$2$/$3$ accounting, however rough, or silence preferred!). Quantified-footprint discipline (measure, however crudely, then improve!) separates design from decoration.
:::

<a id="worked-example"></a>
## 3. Worked Example / Step-by-Step Scenario

::: step [Step 1: Setup] Formulating the Problem
"Solar dryer for fish vendors: (a) triple-line audit? (b) TRL today (working lab prototype!) + evidence to reach next rung? (c) Greenwash-proof claims?"
:::

::: step [Step 2: Execution] Audit, Rung, Proof
1. (a) Planet (solar vs firewood smoke + plastic reduction!), people (women vendors' time/income!), profit (payback math vs firewood spend!).
2. (b) TRL $4$ (lab-validated!) → $5/6$ needs *relevant-environment* demo (harbour-side pilot, monsoon included!) with measured throughput/moisture curves.
3. (c) Numbers only (kg-firewood-avoided metered! kWh metered!) — sticker claims banned pending meters.
:::

::: step [Step 3: Conclusion] Final Result
Triple audit, rung-plus-evidence-to-next, metered-claims-only. Evidence-to-next-rung (what demo unlocks what!) is the TRL answer motion.
:::

<a id="self-check"></a>
## 4. Active Recall Quizzes

::: quiz Q1: Foundational Concept
Valley of death ($TRL 4$–$7$) kills technologies by:
(A) Physics limits
(*B) Funding mismatch (research money ends, scale money wants proof — pilot/demo costs fall *between* mandates!; team/business-model gaps compound!) — bridge instruments (grants, pilots, partnerships!) target the gap explicitly
(C) Bad luck clustering
(D) Peer review harshness
::: explanation
Mandate-gap mechanics (neither-research-nor-product budget owns pilots!) plus team gaps (researchers ≠ scalers!). Gap-named instruments (pilot funds, industry partnerships!) are the policy answer — name the gap, then the bridge.
:::

::: quiz Q2: Foundational Concept
Circular design hierarchies order (refuse/reduce/reuse/recycle) by:
(A) Alphabetical neatness
(*B) Value-retention (refuse > reduce > reuse > recycle > recover > dispose — inner loops preserve embodied value!; recycling *last* resort before recovery, not first virtue!) — design chases inner loops (durability/repair/modularity first!)
(C) Cost ascending
(D) Fashion cycles
::: explanation
Embodied-value logic (keep products whole longest!) — recycling celebrated prematurely excuses disposability (shredding value to recover scraps!). Inner-loop design (repair/modular/upgrade paths!) outranks recyclability claims.
:::

::: quiz Q3: Foundational Concept
TRL inflation (claiming $7$ at lab-demo stage) harms by:
(A) Flattering teams briefly
(*B) Mismatched money/expectations (scale funds for lab toys → field failures!; partner trust burns!) — evidence-graded rungs (demo *where*, measured *what*!) keep claims honest
(C) Nothing, optimism helps
(D) Slowing publications
::: explanation
Rung-evidence pairing (each TRL needs *its* proof type!) — inflated claims misroute resources (pilot cash for bench curiosities!). Evidence-per-rung discipline (relevant-environment proof for $5$–$6$!) is the honesty mechanism.
:::
