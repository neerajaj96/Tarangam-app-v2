# Social Impact, HCD & Inclusive Design

**Whose problems count — societal needs, human-centred bridging, and designing with (not for) diverse users.**

<a id="the-intuition"></a>
## 1. The Intuition

::: callout-intuition Core Mental Model: Bridge-Building, Not Gift-Dropping
**Social impact** starts from *their* map (multi-faceted issues: health/income/dignity entangled! — single-lens fixes snap!). **HCD** bridges designer-world and user-world (live in their shoes *before* sketching — empathy as method, not mood!). **Inclusive design** widens the bridge (age/disability/language/literacy variance *designed in* — curb-cut effect: ramps help strollers *and* wheelchairs, everyone wins!). Tech meeting society (not parachuting onto it!) is the thesis — community engagement from day zero.
:::

<a id="the-math"></a>
## 2. Theoretical Framework & Formalism

### 2.1 Needs-to-bridge pipeline + inclusion mechanics

* Societal-needs reading (multi-faceted mapping! community engagement protocols! empathetic design stances!).
* HCD divide-bridging (designer assumptions vs lived realities — immersion closes!).
* Inclusion mechanics (user-diversity matrices! edge-user first design (solve hardest, help all!)! long-term impact lenses!).

::: callout-formula KTU Formula Vault: Impact Pipeline
Map needs (multi-faceted!) → immerse (HCD!) → design edges-first (inclusion!) → measure long-term.
:::

::: callout-pitfall Parachute Innovation (Gift-Drop Failure!)
Designed-elsewhere solutions *dropped* on communities (no engagement, no maintenance story!) rot on arrival — adoption needs ownership (co-design!), maintenance needs locals (capacity, not charity!). Engagement-depth audit per project (who decided what, when?) exposes parachutes.
:::

<a id="worked-example"></a>
## 3. Worked Example / Step-by-Step Scenario

::: step [Step 1: Setup] Formulating the Problem
"Low-cost water filter for a fishing hamlet: (a) multi-faceted need map? (b) HCD immersion plan (week 1!)? (c) Edge-first inclusion picks? (d) Parachute-risk audit?"
:::

::: step [Step 2: Execution] Needs to Anti-Parachute
1. (a) Facets: health (waterborne illness!) + income (fishing days lost!) + dignity (women's collection burden!) + ecology (lagoon plastic!) — entangled, all named.
2. (b) Week 1: dawn collection walk-alongs + tea-stall chats + catch-landing observations (live rhythms before sketching!).
3. (c) Edges first: arthritic grip strength (maintenance handles!), non-literate instructions (pictorial!), monsoon-proofing (seasonal edge!).
4. (d) Audit: who specified flow-rate (lab or hamlet?)! who repairs month 13 (trained local + spares chain?)! who decided price (cooperative say?)! — engagement receipts per decision.
:::

::: step [Step 3: Conclusion] Final Result
Facet-map, immersion-week, edge-picks, engagement-receipts. Receipt-per-decision (who-decided-with-whom evidence!) is the anti-parachute proof format.
:::

## 4. Active Recall Quizzes

::: quiz Q1: Foundational Concept
Curb-cut effect argues for inclusive design economically by:
(A) Charity optics
(*B) Edge-solutions generalise (ramps→strollers/trolleys/tourists! captions→gyms/offices!) — designing hardest-first *expands* markets (ROI, not alms!) plus compliance-plus-brand dividends
(C) Regulations only
(D) Slower timelines accepted
::: explanation
Generalisation dividend (edge→mainstream spillover!) reframes inclusion as growth strategy (bigger addressable base!). Dividend examples per domain (type the spillover!) make the business case concrete.
:::

::: quiz Q2: Foundational Concept
Multi-faceted issue mapping prevents:
(A) Slow projects
(*B) Single-lens fixes that snap others (pure-tech filter ignoring collection-burden/income-links/maintenance-ecology! — systems view before solution shape!)
(C) Community meetings
(D) Documentation
::: explanation
Entanglement-first mapping (health×income×dignity×ecology joints!) routes holistic design (solution must clear *all* facets!). Facet-check per proposal (which facets does it serve/harm?) is the review gate.
:::

::: quiz Q3: Foundational Concept
"Nothing about us without us" operationalised in HCD means:
(A) Slogans on slides
(*B) Decision rights shared (users co-specify/co-test/co-own — engagement receipts: attendance *plus* decision logs showing user input *changing* designs!) — participation with power, not presence
(C) Longer workshops
(D) Free products for all
::: explanation
Power-sharing evidence (logged design changes traceable to user input!) distinguishes co-design from consultation-theatre (heard-then-ignored!). Receipt discipline (decision→whose-input→what-changed!) audits participation honesty.
:::
